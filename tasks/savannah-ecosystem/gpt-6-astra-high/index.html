NOTES FROM THE GRASSLANDS

Meet your neighbours.

Three species. One interconnected landscape.

01

The lion Panthera leo

Life moves at the pace of the pride. Watch three lions patrol together, slow down in the heat, and settle into the grass.

02

The elephant Loxodonta africana

A close-knit herd of three follows the matriarch. They pause to drink, raise their trunks, and stay closer in the dry season.

03

The leopard Panthera pardus

Two solitary explorers slip through the grass. Look for their quick, low stride and an occasional climb into an acacia.

Seasons turn every 60 seconds of active time. Rain brings greener grass and more activity; storms send wildlife searching for shelter. Snow is an imaginative dry-season experiment.
