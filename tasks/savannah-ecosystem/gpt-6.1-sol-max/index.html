NOTES FROM THE GRASSLANDS

Every life has
its own rhythm.

A little world, drawn entirely in code. Watch the herds, change the skies, and see how everything responds.

01

The pride AFRICAN LION

Social, unhurried wanderers. Lions patrol together and take generous breaks in the heat. Storms bring the pride closer.

02

The herd AFRICAN ELEPHANT

Gentle giants with strong family bonds. Look for a raised trunk or a pause at the waterhole. The wet season adds a spring to their step.

03

The solitary ones AFRICAN LEOPARD

Quiet, independent travelers. They move in quick, low strides and sometimes climb an acacia for a better view.

Seasons turn every 60 simulation seconds. Snow is a rare, imaginative dry-season event. Lightning always finds a clear corridor; the tornado follows the nearest animal at a slower pace.
