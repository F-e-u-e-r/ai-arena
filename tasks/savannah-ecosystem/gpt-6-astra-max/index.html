THE SAVANNA FIELD GUIDE

      
A world with
its own rhythm.

      

        Eight neighbours. Two seasons. An ever-changing sky. This small
        landscape is alive with little decisions.
      

      

        

          Lions stay close to their pride, amble across the
          plains, and settle down for a well-earned rest.
        

        

          Elephants follow their family, stop by the waterhole,
          and raise their trunks to greet the air.
        

        

          Leopards prefer their own company. Look for a quick,
          quiet stride or a retreat into an acacia tree.
        

      

      

        Seasons turn every 60 seconds. The rains bring greener grass and more
        activity; the dry season draws families closer. Storms send the animals
        hurrying, and lightning always finds a clear path. Snow is an
        imaginative highland mode available in the dry season.
