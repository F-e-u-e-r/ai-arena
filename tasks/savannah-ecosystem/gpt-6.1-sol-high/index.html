THE WILDFIELD FIELD GUIDE

Meet your wild neighbors.

Six lives, one shared landscape. Select an animal in the scene to follow its rhythm.

01

African elephant

Loxodonta africana

A family affair. Our elephants move as a herd, pause by the waterhole to drink, and lift their trunks to sense their surroundings.

02

African lion

Panthera leo

The art of taking it slowly. Lions patrol together, punctuating their unhurried journey with short rests in the grass.

03

African leopard

Panthera pardus pardus

A solitary wanderer. Leopards move quickly and quietly, occasionally climbing an acacia for a different view of the world.

The landscape has a rhythm, too. Seasons turn every 60 simulation seconds. Wet seasons bring greener grass and livelier animals; dry seasons draw the herds together. Snow is a rare, playful dry-season possibility, rather than typical Serengeti weather.
